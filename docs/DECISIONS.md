# Design and technical decisions

- **Editorial light theme.** Keep the mostly white/off-white surface, near-black text, generous space, restrained blue/sage/lavender, and system font stack. This follows the agreed Bradley Ziffer-inspired direction while retaining an original portfolio identity.
- **Name-first hero.** “shota kakiuchi.” is the main heading; “mechanical & hardware engineer.” is smaller. Previous greeting/kicker text is superseded and must not return. Hero height stays flexible, especially on small screens.
- **Home and navigation.** Keep SK. and About / Projects / Resume / Contact. “Explore my work” targets selected work; Projects opens the full collection. Philosophy and About business card remain.
- **Homepage hierarchy.** Selected Work remains the first major homepage section. A compact four-card At a Glance overview follows it and links to the detailed About sections; it complements rather than replaces the full About-page bento grid.
- **About section navigation.** `src/data/aboutSections.ts` is the shared source for the six About anchors and their display numbers in both the page contents navigation and the global About flyout. The R&D process belongs between Experience and Expertise, preserving the established 01–06 sequence.
- **About opening.** Keep the exact large “a little / about me.” heading and mark only “about me.” with a restrained powder-blue underline. Place a compact native At a Glance disclosure to its right and directly above the existing business card, keeping the introduction and card in matching columns on wide screens and stacking them in reading order on smaller screens. The full About BentoGrid remains in the codebase but is not squeezed into this compact panel.
- **Motion and color.** Use a one-time shared typing reveal for primary page titles and the Home subtitle, with static screen-reader text and an immediate static presentation for reduced-motion users. Keep motion subtle and use route-specific pastel marker accents sparingly within the light editorial palette.
- **Page introductions.** Home, About, Projects, Contact, and Resume share the `PageIntro.astro` / `AnimatedPageTitle.astro` title typography, punctuation, and `--page-intro-top` alignment. Home keeps its distinct name-first composition. About navigation separates the direct page link from its chevron submenu control.
- **Expertise claims.** About-page expertise, toolkit, and industry content are structured data rendered from `src/data/profile.ts`. Requested skills lacking project-specific or professional evidence remain in a visible review list rather than being represented as established expertise.
- **Resume behavior.** Resume navigation points to `/resume/`, with readable embedded preview and Open PDF fallback. Download is separate and optional. Do not put `download` on the primary preview action.
- **Illustration versus evidence.** Homepage and collection listing media are simplified concept illustrations, labeled as such. The preserved ECM SVG remains a style reference; the active ECM cover is the owner-supplied ECM concept image. Photographs, detailed CAD, report pages, diagrams, and test results stay inside their case studies, where captions preserve context. This replaces the earlier mixed listing covers and the rocket airbrake-CAD listing cover.
- **Supplied gimbal and Mini Pupper covers.** The owner-supplied red gimbal and yellow robot-dog illustrations are the active listing covers for their respective projects. Their baked interface labels were removed in repository copies; `ProjectCard.astro` remains the single source of the card caption and arrow.
- **Content source of truth.** Profile arrays and site/contact values live in `src/data/`; project facts and case studies live in Markdown collection entries; document metadata lives in `src/data/documents.ts`. Avoid redundant hard-coded copies.
- **Static hosting.** Keep Astro static output and existing GitHub Pages workflow. No backend or paid runtime is needed; do not publish or push without authorization.
- **Draft handling.** Preserve collection `status` behavior so drafts do not gain public routes.

If a new request conflicts with these decisions, follow the site owner’s explicit instruction and update this record. The unresolved rocket cover request is documented as incomplete rather than treated as an approved visual change.
