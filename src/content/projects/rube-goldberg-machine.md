## Problem and constraints

The collaborative machine transported a ball through 40 sections, each demonstrating mechanisms and engineering principles. The sequence links individual sections into a complete mechanical build.

## My contribution

I contributed to designing and building the collaborative Rube Goldberg machine. The 40-section count describes the complete collaborative machine, rather than 40 sections attributed to my individual work.

## System design and implementation

The ball is the link between successive mechanisms. Each section transfers it into the next, making interfaces between sections and coordination across the build central to the project.

The photograph and annotated CAD show a mechanism from the existing project portfolio. They document part of the collaborative build, rather than all 40 sections.
